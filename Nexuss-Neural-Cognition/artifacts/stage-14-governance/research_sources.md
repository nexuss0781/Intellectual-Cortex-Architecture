# Stage 14 Governance Research Sources

The Stage 14 protocol uses the following authoritative materials as design references for lifecycle governance, continuous monitoring, secure development, prompt-injection controls, and provider-level governance. These references inform the control contracts; they do not constitute an independent assessment of Nexuss, a compliance determination, or production authorization.

## NIST AI Risk Management Framework

The NIST AI RMF provides a voluntary framework for incorporating trustworthiness considerations into the design, development, use, and evaluation of AI systems. Stage 14 maps its governance and measurement intent into release ledgers, monitoring records, incident response, claims renewal, and explicit evidence boundaries.

Source: [NIST AI Risk Management Framework][1]

## NIST Generative AI Profile

The NIST Generative AI Profile is used as a design reference for generative-AI-specific risk categories, including safety, privacy, security, misinformation, and lifecycle management. Stage 14 keeps these domains separate rather than collapsing them into one quality number.

Source: [NIST Generative AI Profile][2]

## NIST Secure Software Development Profile

The NIST secure-development profile informs the requirement for controlled changes, provenance, approval, evaluation, rollback, and audit-ready release records.

Source: [NIST AI Secure Software Development Profile][3]

## OWASP Prompt-Injection Guidance

OWASP prompt-injection guidance informs the adversarial drills and the denial of malicious tool proposals, credential exfiltration, and unbounded RPC behavior. The harness validates a narrow control contract; it does not establish comprehensive security against all prompt-injection attacks.

Source: [OWASP LLM01:2025 Prompt Injection][4]

## European Commission Provider Guidance

The European Commission provider guidance and AI Code of Practice are included as lifecycle and risk-governance references for claims, transparency, safety, and provider responsibilities. They are not used as a legal or compliance conclusion for this repository.

Sources: [GPAI Provider Guidance][5] [AI Code of Practice][6]

## Evidence limitation

All Stage 14 results are generated by deterministic repository-local contracts and bounded simulations. No external reviewer, real region, real tenant, public service, or production monitoring system participated in this preparation run.
